# Active Skills

Active skills are everything you trigger yourself: weapon strikes, dashes, spins, deployed devices, and consumables. They sit on the skill toolbar, four slots by default (the Battery Pack passive adds a fifth), and are bound to their own keys or gamepad buttons.

## Weapons

The Seeker carries melee and ranged weapons. The **sword** and **hammer** are melee weapons with their own reach, weight, and knockback; the hammer hits harder but slows you down. The **bow** fires arrows, which are a crafted resource. Weapon attacks have directional variants, so striking up, down, or forward while running, jumping, or falling all do what you'd expect.

## How skills are earned

| Source | Skills |
| --- | --- |
| From the start | Weapon attack and Tether Node |
| **Adaptation**: experience from defeated creatures raises it level by level | Dash, Whirl, Stealth, Dash Strike, Flicker Strike |
| **Capsule crafting**: research recipes with Essence, then craft them from materials | Bow, Explosive Mine, Spinner drone, Ice Nova, Quark Pylon, Amplified Bell, Portable Tent |
| **Foraging** | Red, Blue, and Moss Berries, eaten for their effects |

Deployable skills such as mines, pylons, bells, tents, and tether nodes use stock: each use spends one from your backpack.

## Abilities taken from creatures

Some skills come straight from the planet's creatures. **Dash Strike** borrows the bat's dive: you plunge through enemies and burst on landing. **Spit** goes further: the Seeker briefly takes spider form and spits venom at the nearest foe. Spit and the fiery **Burning Dash** are still prototypes outside the regular progression.

## Design principles

- **Flow over interruption.** Attacks should blend with movement instead of rooting you in place, unless stopping is the point of the skill.
- **Combine freely.** Skills should synergize with each other, with passives, and with weapons, with as few incompatible combinations as possible.
- **Every skill is a playstyle.** Stealth rewards patience and backstabs, Whirl and Flicker Strike reward diving into crowds, and devices reward setting up the battlefield.

## Where it's heading

The goal is for **every creature ability to become learnable**, and not just as a projectile fired from an unchanged body: the Seeker can temporarily become the creature, the way Spit already turns them into a spider. As creatures adapt to how you fight, their new forms become learnable too, so the toolbox keeps growing alongside the planet. See [Creatures and Ecosystem](creatures-ecosystem.md).
