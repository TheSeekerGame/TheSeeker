# Platforming

Movement should feel precise, responsive, and expressive, in the spirit of Hollow Knight. Getting around the planet is meant to be a pleasure in its own right.

## Movement today

- **Variable jump height.** Release the jump button early for a short hop; hold it for a full jump.
- **Coyote time.** You can still jump for a moment after running off a ledge.
- **Wall slides and wall jumps.** Slide down walls and kick off them. Touching a wall restores your jump, so you can climb shafts by bouncing between walls.
- **Dash.** Bursts you past danger. Like every ability outside the starting kit, it's copied from a creature that uses it. Dash Strike dives straight down through enemies.
- **Extra jumps.** The Rabbit's Foot passive adds a double jump.
- **Capsule lifts.** Jump inside a capsule and drop through its hatch to ride the lift down.

Spikes hurt and send you back to the last safe ground you stood on, so a mistake costs health and a little time rather than a long walk back.

## The caves as a course

Each world is assembled from validated chunk plans, so it's new every time while its routes stay traversable. Broad chambers give room for fights; shafts, ledges, and wall-supported climbs test movement between them. Secret rooms hide behind breakable walls, and the planet can grow new spikes into ground you've already crossed.

## Design principles

- **Platforming is optional depth.** Difficult platforming should guard hidden areas and rewards, never the main journey. Being good at platforming should pay off without being required to enjoy the game.
- **Movement and combat don't fight each other.** You should be able to attack while running, jumping, and falling without losing control of the Seeker.
- **Rhythm.** Movement lives on the same musical clock as the rest of the game, so steps, jumps, and landings fall into the soundtrack's pulse.

## Where it's heading

Optional platforming challenges are meant to become a bigger part of exploration, guarding hidden areas and rare materials. Learned creature forms should bring their own movement too: becoming a creature can mean moving the way it does.
