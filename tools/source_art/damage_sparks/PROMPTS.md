# Damage Spark Source Prompts

Mode for all three assets: `stylized-concept`.

The extracted reference-video contact sheet was provided as a style and motion
reference, not as source artwork to reproduce.

## Sword

> Use case: stylized-concept
> Asset type: high-resolution source art for a 2D game damage-impact sprite animation
>
> Create one horizontal three-frame sword hit spark on a genuine transparent
> background. Read the frames left to right: frame 1 is a compact ignition,
> frame 2 is the widest slash, and frame 3 is a rapid collapse. Orient the
> effect along local +X. Use a compact asymmetrical white core, one dominant
> tapered slash/ray axis, two or three shorter rays, and a few detached flecks.
> Use white and pale cool gray with only a subtle cool-blue edge. Make three
> equal square cells with ample transparent padding. No text, characters,
> weapons, scenery, circular shockwave, lens flare, smoke, checkerboard, border,
> grid, or watermark.

## Projectile

> Use case: stylized-concept
> Asset type: high-resolution source art for a 2D game damage-impact sprite animation
> The referenced image is only a motion/style reference for the brief white sword impact spark. Create a new projectile impact spark animation, not a copy.
>
> Produce one horizontal strip of exactly three equal square animation frames, read left to right. Each cell must contain one small, narrow, sharp projectile-hit spark centered in the cell and oriented along local +X (left-to-right travel): frame 1 is a tiny point ignition, frame 2 is the peak impact with a thin tapered horizontal needle/ray and two very short asymmetric splinters, frame 3 rapidly contracts to a few tiny flecks. Keep the effect substantially smaller, thinner, and sharper than a sword slash or hammer impact. Use a bright white core, pale cool-gray secondary pixels, and only a very restrained cool-blue edge. Keep ample empty padding around every frame. No weapon, projectile body, enemy, character, scenery, smoke, circular shockwave, lens flare, text, border, grid, watermark, or checkerboard.
>
> CRITICAL OUTPUT REQUIREMENT: the background must be genuine transparent alpha (RGBA). Do not draw or simulate a checkerboard. Every area outside the spark must have alpha 0. The three frame cells must align perfectly and have equal dimensions.
